Heart of the Village

The Story

In a world of small, creative villagers, peace is threatened by oversized invaders. Lacking weapons, you must use handmade towers crafted from everyday tools to defend the Heart of the Village.

Game Rules

  • Goal: Stop enemies from reaching the end of the path.
  • Build: Click on grass tiles to spend money and place towers.
  • Upgrade: Click on existing towers to make them stronger.
  • Win/Lose: Survive all waves to win. If your Health reaches 0, you lose.

Controls

  • P: Pause Game
  • Z: Mute/Unmute Music
  • 1: Select Arrow Tower
  • 2: Select Bomb Tower
  • 3: Select Magic Tower
  • 4: Select Flame Tower
  • Escape: Deselect Tower
  • N: Start Next Wave
  • M: Auto Start Waves
  • F: Change Game Speed

Your Defenses

  • Arrow: Fast fire rate, single target.
  • Cannon: High damage, slow speed.
  • Magic: Splash damage.
  • Flame: High fire rate, low damage.

The Threats

  • Fast: Low HP, high speed.
  • Tank: High HP, slow speed.
  • Boss: The final challenge.
